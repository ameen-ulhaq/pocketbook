Help and tips

Where is my data?
Only on this phone. Make a backup file from More, Backup and export, and keep it somewhere safe.
I got a new phone
Install Pocketbook, tap “Have a backup? Restore it” on the first screen, and choose your backup file.
A transfer is not spending
Moving money between your own accounts changes balances only. It never shows in reports.
Someone paid me back
Open Debts, tap the person, then Record a payment. It is not counted as income.
I forgot my PIN
There is no reset, because nothing is stored online. Use Face ID, or erase and restore a backup.
